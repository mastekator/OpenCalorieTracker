Spec for the developer

Colours (tokens.css, --logo-*)

Tile --logo-tile · #E5F5EC Pod --logo-pod · #13784A Protein pea --logo-protein · #4766C4 Fat pea --logo-fat · #B36F00 Carbs pea --logo-carbs · #16847E

On dark grounds the standalone mark uses --logo-*-on-dark (the dark-theme accent and macro colours). Pea order is always protein · fat · carbs, left to right — the same order as on Today.

Clear space and sizes

  • Clear space: one pea diameter (≈ 20 % of the mark) on every side.
  • Minimum: 16 px as the favicon tile, 24 px for the bare mark.
  • Don't recolour the peas, rotate the pod or put the bare mark on the green accent.

Assets

  • icon.svg — square tile, no rounding: source for every PNG.
  • icon-180.png — apple-touch-icon.
  • icon-192.png, icon-512.png — manifest icons, purpose: "any maskable" (the mark is inside the safe zone).
  • favicon.svg + favicon-32.png fallback.
  • mark.svg, mark-dark.svg — splash and in-app use.
  • Manifest background_color = color-bg (#F8FAF9), theme_color = --theme-color of each theme.
  • README: icon-512.png shown at 96 px + the name as an H1.
