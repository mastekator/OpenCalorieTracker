# Roadmap

The first release (v0.1.0) covers the core loop: photo → recognized dishes → edit → diary, the daily goal, multiple users, offline PWA, English and Russian.

What comes after it. **Plans may change** — order and scope depend on feedback.

## Next modules

- **Water and drinks** — log water and drinks, with their calories counted in the day.
- **Vitamins and supplements** — keep track of what you take and when.
- **Dietary restrictions** — set your diet (vegetarian, allergies and more) once and see it respected across the app.
- **Recipes and fridge** — save your own recipes and get ideas from what you have at home.
- **Period report** — a look back over weeks or months: trends, averages, progress toward the goal.
- **Streaks, badges and challenges** — light motivation to keep the diary going.

## Diary and sync

- **Photo of a saved entry** — attach or keep a photo for entries added later.
- **Edit conflicts** — a clear choice when the same entry was changed on two devices offline.

## AI

- **Per-user AI** — each user can bring their own API key or AI subscription, with explicit consent before photos are sent.

## Integrations

- Health apps (Apple Health, Health Connect).
- Import and export of your data.
- Barcode scanning.
- Webhooks and an MCP server for your own automations and AI assistants.

## Apps and website

- **Mobile app** for iOS and Android.
- **Project website and a live demo.**
